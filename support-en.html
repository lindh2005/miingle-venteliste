Frequently asked questions

I have forgotten my password

Tap "Forgot password?" on the login screen and enter your email. You will receive a code by email, which you enter in the app, and then you can choose a new password.

How do I cancel Miingle Plus?

The subscription is bought and cancelled in the App Store: open Settings on your iPhone → your name → Subscriptions → Miingle → Cancel Subscription. You keep Plus until the end of the period you have paid for. On Android, cancel in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.

Can I get a refund for Miingle Plus?

Miingle Plus is paid through the App Store, so refund requests must be sent to Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot refund a subscription charged by Apple ourselves, but do write to us if we can help.

What about tickets for walks and talks?

Tickets are paid through Stripe, and we refund them ourselves: if we cancel, you always get your money back. Talks are refunded if you cancel more than 24 hours before the start. A walk ticket is not refunded when you cancel yourself, but you can use it for the same walk again. Read more in the terms.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile → Settings → Delete my account in the app. All your data is deleted permanently, and paid tickets for walks and talks starting more than 24 hours later are refunded automatically. Remember to cancel any subscription in the App Store as well.

How do I report a user or a message?

Tap the report button next to the user, message or group in the app. We review all reports as quickly as possible. If it is urgent, also write to support@miingle.dk.
