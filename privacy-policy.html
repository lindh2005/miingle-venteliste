Data controller

Miingle ApS is the data controller for the information collected about users of the app, people on our waiting list, visitors to our website and other parties. We make sure that personal data is processed in accordance with the legislation in force at any given time.

Our contact details are:

  • Miingle ApS
  • CVR no. (Danish company registration number) 41785942
  • Address: Greve Main 42, 2670 Greve, Denmark
  • Email: privacy@miingle.dk

Your profile and use of the app

When you, as a parent or guardian, create a profile on the platform in order to meet and network with other parents across the community, we process information about you and your child to facilitate contact between users and support the purpose of the platform.

When you create and fill in a profile with us, we process ordinary personal data about you, including your name, email address, date of birth, profile picture, whether you are a mother or a father, your district and your language, as well as your children's first names, dates of birth and gender. We use your district to match you with parents and children in the same area. Your phone's precise location (GPS) is only used on your phone to show the distance to activities and is not stored by us.

When you use the app, we also process your friendships, messages, the groups you create or are a member of, your sign-ups for activities and talks, ratings after activities and any warnings for late cancellation, no-shows or reports that a participant was not the person shown in the profile.

When you make a purchase, we process information about the purchase: the Miingle Plus subscription is paid via the App Store or Google Play, and tickets for walks and talks are paid via our payment provider Stripe. We do not receive your card details.

The purpose of this processing is to deliver our services in accordance with the agreement entered into, including administering the platform's features, for example supporting networks and social communities by facilitating contact between parents and children in connection with activities, talks and other events on the platform. The legal basis for this processing is therefore the performance of the contract you enter into with us, cf. GDPR Article 6(1)(b).

The processing of your children's first names and ages is based on our legitimate interest, cf. GDPR Article 6(1)(f). Our legitimate interest is, overall, to serve the purpose of Miingle and connect you and your children with parents and children of interest.

You can delete your profile at any time in the app under Profile → Settings → Delete my account. This deletes your profile, your children's information, your messages, friendships and sign-ups. However, we keep information about purchases for as long as the Danish Bookkeeping Act requires.

We keep your personal data for as long as your user profile is active and it is necessary to provide the platform's features. If the data is no longer necessary for the purpose, it is deleted or anonymised. If you have not used the app for 3 years, we delete your profile in the same way as if you had deleted it yourself. We will email you 30 days in advance, and if you open the app before then, you keep your profile. Profiles with an active subscription are not deleted. Accounting records are kept for 5 years from the end of the financial year to which they relate. Data processed on the basis of your consent is deleted if you withdraw your consent.

Your group

If you sign up for Your group – small groups for mothers in the same district – we use your district, the fact that you are a mother and your youngest child's date of birth (or that you are expecting) to place you in a group with other mothers whose children are roughly the same age. Members of the group can see your name and profile picture and write with you in the group chat, where Miingle may also post icebreaker questions.

We keep a record of which groups you have been in, so that you are not placed back in a group you have left, and we never place two people who have blocked each other in the same group.

Signing up is voluntary, and the processing takes place to deliver the feature you have asked for, cf. GDPR Article 6(1)(b). The data is deleted together with your profile.

Articles and reading history

When you have read an article in the app, we store that you have read it so that we can show »✓ Read« next to the article. Some articles cover topics that may be personal, such as children with special needs. We therefore only use your reading history for this display – not for marketing, profiling or statistics – and other users cannot see it.

The processing is based on our legitimate interest in making the app easy to use, cf. GDPR Article 6(1)(f). Your reading history is deleted together with your profile.

The waiting list

When you sign up for the waiting list at venteliste.miingle.dk, we process your email address, the time of your sign-up and confirmation and – if you came via a special link, for example from a flyer, Instagram or a partner – which channel you came from.

We use your email address to confirm your sign-up, send you emails about the launch and your personal discount code, and to determine whether you can take part in the prize draw (see the section on prize draws). We send emails about the launch on the basis of your consent, cf. GDPR Article 6(1)(a), and section 10 of the Danish Marketing Practices Act. We use the channel information in aggregate to see which marketing works, on the basis of our legitimate interest, cf. GDPR Article 6(1)(f).

You can unsubscribe at any time via the link in our emails or by writing to privacy@miingle.dk. We delete the waiting list no later than 6 months after the app has launched. If you create a profile in the app, the sections about the app apply to your data there.

Our waiting list website does not use cookies, statistics or tracking.

Enquiries

If you contact us by email or in connection with other enquiries, we process ordinary personal data about you in the form of your name, email address, address, workplace, job title and any other personal data that appears in your enquiry.

We process your personal data on the basis of GDPR Article 6(1)(f) on legitimate interest. Our legitimate interest is to be able to receive, follow up on and answer your enquiry.

As a rule, we keep your enquiry for 1 year. However, there may be circumstances that mean we keep your enquiry for longer.

Content on social media and our website

We post on social media such as Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn etc. and on our website in order to inform about our services and show feedback from users and partners. In this connection, we process ordinary personal data about participants in the posts in the form of name, possibly workplace, job title and picture.

We process this personal data on the basis of GDPR Article 6(1)(a) on consent.

We delete the data on an ongoing basis and no later than 5 years after the content was published. However, special circumstances may entitle or oblige us to keep the data for a longer period.

Reporting or blocking

When you use the report or block button in the app, we process the personal data needed to handle your report or block. This may include information about your profile and the user concerned, the reported content, your reason and the time of the report or block. The purpose is to carry out the block, investigate the report and take appropriate measures, for example removing content or restricting a user's access to the app.

The processing is based on GDPR Article 6(1)(f). Our legitimate interest is to maintain safety and decency in the app, protect users from offensive or abusive content and behaviour, and enforce our terms of use. We only process the data to the extent necessary for these purposes and where your interests or fundamental rights and freedoms do not override our legitimate interest. To the extent that the processing is necessary to comply with a specific legal obligation, it is based on GDPR Article 6(1)(c).

We keep the data for as long as necessary to handle the report, maintain the block and follow up on any violations. After that, the data is deleted unless continued storage is necessary to comply with a legal obligation or to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

Marketing

If you sign up for our newsletter, we process your email address to send you newsletters with news and marketing within the topics covered by your consent. The processing is based on your consent, cf. GDPR Article 6(1)(a). Newsletters with marketing are sent on the basis of your prior consent under section 10(1) of the Danish Marketing Practices Act.

Signing up for the newsletter is voluntary. You can withdraw your consent at any time, easily and free of charge, via the unsubscribe link in every newsletter. We will then stop sending you newsletters. Withdrawing your consent does not affect the lawfulness of the processing carried out on the basis of your consent before it was withdrawn.

Prize draws

If you take part in a prize draw, we process your email address and the information needed to determine whether you meet the conditions for taking part, to draw the winners and to contact them. If you win a prize from one of our partners, we pass your name and email address on to the partner – solely so that the prize can be delivered. The processing takes place to fulfil the terms of the prize draw, cf. GDPR Article 6(1)(b).

We delete the information about your participation when the prize draw has ended and the prizes have been delivered, unless we need to keep it longer to comply with a legal obligation.

Push notifications

We send push notifications about, for example, messages, friends, your sign-ups and new activities and communities in your district. This is based on our legitimate interest in giving you relevant information about your use of Miingle, cf. GDPR Article 6(1)(f). You choose in the app which types of notifications you want.

Notifications about offers and partner benefits are marketing and are only sent if you have given your consent on the consent screen or under Profile → Settings → Privacy and consent, cf. GDPR Article 6(1)(a). To make the content relevant, we use your district and your children's ages – not your precise location. You can withdraw your consent at any time in the same place in the app.

App technology and updates

When you use our app, we process technical information in order to run, secure and improve the app:

  • Error and crash reports (e.g. device type, operating system, app version and time) via Sentry, which is located in the EU. This is based on our legitimate interest in being able to find and fix errors, cf. GDPR Article 6(1)(f). The reports are deleted after 30 days.
  • Usage statistics via PostHog, which is located in the EU – only if you have given your consent on the consent screen or under Profile → Settings → Privacy and consent, cf. GDPR Article 6(1)(a). The statistics are used to improve the app and are deleted after 1 year.
  • A push token, so that we can send you the notifications you have chosen.
  • An anonymous count of clicks on our partners' links and benefits. We do not store who clicked.

We do not use ads, remarketing or tracking across other companies' apps and websites, and we do not share your data with advertisers.

Ongoing updates of Miingle generally take place without processing personal data. Should your personal data exceptionally be involved in connection with updates, operations etc., this processing will be based on our legitimate interest, cf. GDPR Article 6(1)(f). Our legitimate interest is to offer and develop a platform that enables parents to find, get in touch with and share experiences with other parents in similar situations, including parents in their local area. The same applies to any use of AI solutions, which as a rule also takes place without the use of personal data, using only anonymous data.

See more in our cookie policy, which also sets out storage and deletion periods.

How do we process your data?

In order to offer you our services and a good app, we need to process your personal data by collecting, recording, organising, storing, using, saving, deleting, transferring and disclosing it.

Recipients or categories of recipients

We may disclose or entrust your personal data to our data processors, advisers (e.g. lawyers), other external suppliers or partners who assist us with various services, third-party solutions or the running of our business, as well as public authorities, including the Danish Tax Agency and the courts. We never sell your personal data.

Our data processors include Supabase (database, EU), PostHog (usage statistics, EU), Sentry (error reports, EU), Stripe (payment for tickets), RevenueCat (subscription management), Apple and Google (App Store and Google Play), Expo (push notifications), Google Workspace (email), Resend (sending emails), DeepL (translation of our own content – never users' messages), Google Maps (address look-ups), Cloudflare (protecting login against abuse) and GitHub (hosting our waiting list website).

Transfer of personal data to third countries

In some cases, we transfer your personal data to recipients in third countries. This happens when we use data processors outside the EU or EEA. The transfer basis is the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or other appropriate safeguards under Article 46 GDPR, where the European Commission has not adopted an adequacy decision for the third country. You can contact privacy@miingle.dk if you would like information on how to obtain a copy of the relevant transfer basis or where it has been made available.

Security

We store the information you give us, or that we receive about you from others, securely and confidentially. Technically, we always use data processors that we have ensured offer the same level of security for your data as we do, and internally we focus on a high level of data security. Where appropriate, we encrypt, pseudonymise or anonymise your personal data.

We have taken appropriate technical and organisational measures against your personal data being accidentally or unlawfully deleted, published, lost, impaired or disclosed to unauthorised persons, misused or otherwise processed in breach of the legislation in force at any given time.

We delete your personal data when we no longer need to process it to fulfil one or more of the purposes mentioned above. Special rules in legislation, such as the Danish Bookkeeping Act, the Danish Anti-Money Laundering Act and the Danish Limitation Act, may entitle and oblige us to keep it for longer.

Your right to withdraw your consent

You have the right to withdraw your consent to our processing of your personal data at any time. You can do this in the app under Profile → Settings → Privacy and consent, via the unsubscribe link in our emails or by sending an email to privacy@miingle.dk. Withdrawing your consent does not affect the lawfulness of our processing based on your previous consent up until the withdrawal. It therefore only takes effect from that point.

Your rights

You have a number of rights in relation to our processing of your personal data:

  • The right of access
  • The right to rectification
  • The right to erasure
  • The right to restriction of processing
  • The right to object
  • The right to data portability

You can read more about your rights in the Danish Data Protection Agency's guidance on data subjects' rights at www.datatilsynet.dk. If you want to exercise your rights, you can send an email to privacy@miingle.dk.

Complaints to the Danish Data Protection Agency

If you are dissatisfied with our processing of your personal data, you can complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet), Carl Jacobsens Vej 35, 2500 Valby, Denmark, phone +45 33 19 32 00, email dt@datatilsynet.dk.

Any questions?

If you have any questions about our processing of your personal data, you are always welcome to write to privacy@miingle.dk.
