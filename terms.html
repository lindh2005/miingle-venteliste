Free content

As a free user you can see the title, date, time and district of Miingle's own walks and join 3 walks free of charge in total. After that you can join a walk by buying a ticket (DKK 25 per walk) or by subscribing to Miingle Plus. The exact meeting point and route are shown once you have signed up.

Tickets for walks and talks

Tickets for walks and talks are bought in the app and paid through our payment provider Stripe by card or the other payment methods shown at checkout. Miingle does not receive your card details, and you get a receipt by email.

A ticket is for a walk or talk on a specific date. There is therefore no 14-day right of withdrawal on tickets, cf. the Danish Consumer Contracts Act's rules on leisure activities on a specific date. Instead:

  • Talks: If you cancel more than 24 hours before the start, you get the full amount back. If you cancel later, the amount is not refunded, but your place is passed on.
  • Walks: A walk ticket is not refunded when you cancel yourself, but you can sign up for the same walk again with your ticket.
  • If we cancel a walk or a talk, you always get the full amount back.
  • If you get a break from activities (see below), paid walk tickets during the break are refunded.
  • If you delete your account more than 24 hours before a walk or talk you have paid for, you get your money back.

Refunds go back to the payment method you used, usually within 5–10 business days.

Cancellations, warnings and breaks

You can cancel your sign-up for our walks without a warning until 24 hours before the start. If you cancel later, or do not show up without cancelling, you get a warning. Three warnings within 30 days lead to a 7-day break from walks, and your sign-ups during that period are cancelled automatically. We do this because places are limited and the other parents are counting on you.

The break does not cover talks you have paid for. After repeated warnings, however, we may refuse sign-ups for upcoming talks for a period.
